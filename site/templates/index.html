Airport and aviation planning documents

A public library of US airport master plans, Airport Layout Plans (ALPs), and 50-state aviation, land-use, and airport law. Official sources remain the citation of record. Preservation copies live here so the files survive, and so we can show what changed.

Search, airport pages, and coverage maps will land here as the catalog fills in. Until then, the project lives at github.com/alexwitherspoon/aptplans.
