About

AptPlans is a civic library of airport master plans, Airport Layout Plans (ALPs), and state aviation law. An ALP is the FAA drawing set that depicts existing and planned airport facilities; a master plan is the recommended study that usually produces or updates that ALP. Every published document we list keeps a link to the official source and, when we have it, a preservation copy on this service.

Summaries and change notes on this site are unofficial. They are produced by this project to help you find the right page in a long PDF. They are not legal advice, not FAA guidance, and not a substitute for the official file.

If a master plan or ALP is missing, an official URL has died, or a listed file is stale or wrong, open an issue or email contact@aptplans.org
