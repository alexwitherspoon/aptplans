About

A public library of airport plans, layout drawings, and state aviation law.

AptPlans collects airport master plans, Airport Layout Plans ({{ "ALPs"|abbr("alp") }}), and state aviation law. An {{ "ALP"|abbr("alp") }} is the drawing set of today's airport and what is planned next. A master plan is the study that usually produces or updates that {{ "ALP"|abbr("alp") }}. Airport and state pages also list grants we can cite.

Each document links to the official source. When we have it, a saved copy lives here too. Short notes on documents are ours, to help you find a page in a long PDF. Always open the official file.

Missing, dead, or the wrong file? Tell us or email contact@aptplans.org. Subscribe via RSS.
