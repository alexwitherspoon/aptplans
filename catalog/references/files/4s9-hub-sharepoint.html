Mulino State Airport [4S9]

 

Mulino State Airport [4S9]

The Hamlet of Mulino is located 23 miles south of Portland in Clackamas County.

The Airport is owned and operated by the Oregon Department of Aviation.

Major employers in the area include Brentwood Industries and RSG Forest Products. There are logging, road construction, quarrying, and trucking companies to support the timber land managers in the area.  The Airport's runway, Runway 14/32, is 3,425 feet long and 100 feet wide. 



This visual runway is equipped with MIRL and a full length parallel taxiway. The Airport handles approximately 21,300 annual general aviation operations and is home to 63 based aircraft. It is estimated that 39 percent of the Airport's annual operations are conducted by itinerant aircraft.

The 4 seasonal state airports in the Cascade Mountain Range have reopened today!

As of May 1st, the following airports are open for use:
  • Crescent Lake State Airport
  • McKenzie Bridge State Airport
  • Santiam Junction State Airport
  • Toketee State Airport

If you have not flown into these airports before, they're worth a visit! We advise you read all of the published airport information, and reach out to us before flying in.
